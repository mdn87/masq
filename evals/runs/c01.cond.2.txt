```js
for (let i = 0; i <= items.length; i++) {
```

`<=` should be `<`. On the last iteration `i === items.length`, so `items[i]` is `undefined`, and `items[i].price` throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined`. `subtotal` (and `withTax`, which calls it) will throw on any non-empty array.
